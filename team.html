Team

Advancing health through intelligent evidence and living guidelines.

Liang Yao Research Club (ACHIEVE) brings together clinicians, methodologists, and researchers to improve health through intelligent evidence and living guideline enhancement. The mission is to promote high-quality clinical guideline development and evidence synthesis that can directly support more trustworthy, timely, and effective healthcare decisions.

Mission

Focus

Clinical guidance

Support the development of living evidence synthesis and clinical guideline methods that remain current and clinically useful.

Approach

AI + evidence

Combine rigorous methodology, transparent workflows, and AI-assisted analysis to strengthen evidence-based practice.
