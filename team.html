Research team

Advancing clinical evidence through intelligent synthesis and living guidance.

Liang Yao Research Club (ACHIEVE) is a multidisciplinary research group dedicated to improving the quality, timeliness, and actionability of clinical evidence. By integrating clinical expertise, methodological rigor, and digital innovation, the team supports more reliable and evidence-informed decisions in healthcare.

Mission

Health impact

Improve patient care by generating reliable evidence, strengthening guideline development, and translating research into clinically meaningful decisions.

Research agenda

Evidence methods

Advance systematic review, appraisal, and living guideline approaches to keep recommendations current, transparent, and rigorous.

Approach

AI + rigor

Integrate structured review workflows, methodological discipline, and AI-enabled analysis to support clearer and more explainable clinical decisions.

Core focus

Clinical relevance

Ground all research in questions that matter to healthcare practice, patient outcomes, and trustworthy clinical guidance.

Methodological standard

Transparency

Ensure review processes are reproducible, interpretable, and open to scrutiny so evidence can be used with confidence.

Real-world value

Decision support

Translate evidence into practical tools that support clinicians, researchers, and health systems in making better decisions.

Building a stronger evidence-to-practice pipeline.
We aim to create transparent, clinically grounded pathways from literature to recommendation.
