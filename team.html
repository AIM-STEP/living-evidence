Research team

Advancing evidence through intelligent synthesis and living guidance.

Liang Yao Research Club (ACHIEVE) is a multidisciplinary research group focused on improving the quality, timeliness, and actionability of clinical evidence. By combining clinical expertise, methodological rigor, and digital innovation, the team supports better evidence-informed healthcare decisions.

Mission

Health impact

Improve patient care by producing more trustworthy evidence and translating research into clinically meaningful guidance.

Research agenda

Evidence methods

Advance systematic review, appraisal, and living guideline approaches to keep recommendations current, transparent, and rigorous.

Approach

AI + rigor

Integrate structured review workflows and AI-enabled analysis that remain clinically explainable and methodologically sound.

Core focus

Clinical relevance

Ground every project in questions that matter to patient outcomes, healthcare quality, and responsible clinical decision-making.

Standard

Transparency

Ensure review workflows are reproducible, interpretable, and open to scrutiny so evidence can be used with confidence.

Value

Decision support

Translate evidence into practical tools that help clinicians, researchers, and health systems act more effectively.

Building a stronger evidence-to-practice pipeline.
We create transparent, clinically grounded pathways from literature to recommendation.
