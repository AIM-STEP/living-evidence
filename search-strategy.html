    Method & research references

    Build a small set of core concepts from the inclusion criteria. Expand synonyms and spelling variants, combine terms with OR and concepts with AND, then translate field tags and limits for each database. Review the query with an information specialist and test it against known eligible studies before the final search.

    Machine generate displays model-generated word lists for each eligibility criterion, then uses these words with the database choices and limits to draft database-specific queries. Supported date and language limits are applied by this page. This implementation has not been validated as a complete systematic-review search method. Dates and exact executed queries are recorded with each retrieval.

    • Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 4 (updated March 2025) — search concepts, synonyms, controlled vocabulary and peer review.
    • TrialMind: Accelerating clinical evidence synthesis with large language models (npj Digital Medicine, 2025) — staged, human-reviewed evidence synthesis.
    • Chained Prompting for Better Systematic Review Search Strategies (2025 preprint) — concept extraction and expansion before Boolean assembly.
    • Reassessing LLM Boolean Query Generation for Systematic Reviews (SIGIR 2025) — query-generation evaluation and sensitivity to model and prompt choices.
    • Webis-SR4ALL-26: A Large-Scale, Cross-Disciplinary Corpus of Systematic Reviews (April 2026 preprint) — linked search strategies and eligibility criteria for benchmarking.
    • litsearchr — text mining and term co-occurrence for search development.
    • Polyglot Search Translator — translating database-specific search syntax. Systematic Review Accelerator now directs users to TERA.
    • NCBI E-utilities · PubMed 10,000-ID limit · Europe PMC API.
    • Ollama structured outputs · LM Studio structured outputs.
