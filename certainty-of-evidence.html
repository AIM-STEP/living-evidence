Assessment source

Rate the certainty of evidence for each network meta-analysis comparison with GRADE, in four stages: direct evidence, indirect evidence, NMA certainty, then the final effect and certainty. Imprecision is judged once, on the final absolute effect. Every judgement keeps its reason, every computed value shows its formula, and every change is logged.

SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE. All values are invented to show how the tool works. Kept apart from real data and never to be used for a real rating.

Method rules

The GRADE-NMA Workflow and Codebook leave some rules open, and two conflict between the documents. The tool never decides them. Click a rule to see it.
