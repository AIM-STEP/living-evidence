Method and research references

This title/abstract workflow adopts independent pilot voting, criterion-referenced evidence and a reviewable audit trail. It does not implement the full-text paper's embedding model or fuzzy controller. The 3% pilot threshold is a user-selected workflow gate, not a validated stopping rule or a guarantee of recall.

  • Covidence: title and abstract screening — Yes / Maybe / No voting and independent reviewers.
  • Covidence: Maybe votes — uncertain citations advance to full text.
  • Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 4 — over-inclusive title/abstract screening and independent study selection.
  • Rahgozar and Mortezaagha (2026) — traceable criterion evidence and explicit uncertain judgment in a full-text proof of concept; its all-positive pilot did not estimate specificity.
