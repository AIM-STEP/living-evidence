Workflow

A disciplined path from evidence to recommendation.

AIM-STEP turns fragmented literature into an auditable and clinically interpretable evidence pipeline through a sequence of transparent, repeatable steps.

Step 01

Search

Define the clinical question and identify relevant studies.

Step 02

Screen

Apply explicit eligibility criteria to narrow the evidence base.

Step 03

Extract

Capture outcomes, variables, and study characteristics systematically.

Step 04

Appraise

Assess bias, quality, and certainty of evidence with structure.

Step 05

Recommend

Translate findings into clear and clinically usable guidance.

Each stage is designed to improve traceability, reduce ambiguity, and align the final recommendation with both methodological quality and practical healthcare needs.
