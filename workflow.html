Workflow

A structured path from evidence to recommendation.

AIM-STEP turns fragmented literature into an auditable and clinically interpretable evidence pipeline through a sequence of transparent steps.

Step 01

Search

Define the clinical question and identify relevant studies.

Step 02

Screen

Select studies using predefined eligibility criteria.

Step 03

Extract

Capture outcomes, variables, and study characteristics.

Step 04

Appraise

Assess quality, risk of bias, and certainty of evidence.

Step 05

Recommend

Translate findings into clear clinical guidance.
