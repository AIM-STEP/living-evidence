Open-access sources and retrieval settings

Europe PMC and PubMed Central, OpenAlex, Semantic Scholar, Crossref publisher links, Unpaywall, arXiv, and saved full-text links. Retrieval starts automatically while this page is open and idle. Sources are tried in order until a readable file is found; unreadable or mismatched files are skipped. Failed lookups retry after six hours; Download retries immediately. Rate limits, paywalls and scanned PDFs may prevent automatic retrieval. DOI, PMID or title lookups are sent directly to these services. Downloads use your browser and current network without an AI model or local server. Browser cross-origin rules and website access permissions still apply.

Unpaywall requires a real contact email. Leave blank to skip it. This value is sent only with retrieval requests and is not saved.

Pilot full-text screening

TypeSafe screens each criterion using selected full-text passages. Your report passages, criteria and reviewer corrections will be sent to TypeSafe. Keep the Mac Studio connection running.

Your key stays in this tab and is cleared on refresh. Confirm checks account access and loads available models.

  1. Start a round: the AI model reads a random sample of retrieved full texts.
  2. Decide Include, Unclear or Exclude (with the reason) for the same reports. The AI result stays hidden until you finish.
  3. Compare. Your notes on disagreements teach the AI in the next round.
  4. Complete at least one round and confirm corrections. Approve the pilot for formal screening, or run another round. Additional rounds are optional.

Learning from pilot corrections

Confirm the criterion and correction for each disagreement. All corrections are saved; relevant rules and examples are selected for each subsequent request. Both local and API models use this feedback; model weights are not changed. Approval freezes the feedback for formal screening.

        Method and research references

        Full text comes from Europe PMC (open-access JATS XML) or an open-access PDF (read with pdf.js), is cut into sentence windows (four sentences, step two), and each criterion's most relevant passages are found by contrastive semantic highlighting: cosine similarity to the inclusion rule minus similarity to the exclusion rule, with embeddinggemma (keyword scoring when the embedding model is unavailable). The AI judges each numbered criterion from those passages and the abstract (criteria before and after the passages), quoting a passage. An exclusion needs a criterion not met with a quotation found in the full text; the first criterion not met in the reason order is the primary reason. Two readings per report; direct criterion conflicts and unclear reports go to the reviewer; exclusion review is optional. The fuzzy controller of Rahgozar and Mortezaagha is not used: in their evaluation an all-criteria rule failed half of the true includes.

        • Rahgozar and Mortezaagha (2026), Artif Intell Rev: An auditable pipeline for fuzzy full-text screening — chunking, contrastive semantic highlighting, LLM judgment and the audit trail.
        • Cochrane Handbook v6.5.1, Chapter 4 §4.6 — two reviewers at full text (MECIR C39), reasons for exclusion (C41), linking reports (C42), first unmet criterion as the primary reason.
        • PRISMA 2020 — reports sought, not retrieved, assessed, excluded with reasons; studies included.
        • Cao et al. (2025), Ann Intern Med — criteria before and after the article, numbered per-criterion reasoning.
        • Flemyng et al. (2025), position statement on AI in evidence synthesis — human oversight and reporting of AI use.
