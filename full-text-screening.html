    Method and research references

    Full text comes from Europe PMC (open-access JATS XML) or an uploaded PDF (read with pdf.js), is cut into sentence windows (four sentences, step two), and each criterion's most relevant passages are found by contrastive semantic highlighting: cosine similarity to the inclusion rule minus similarity to the exclusion rule, with embeddinggemma (keyword scoring when the embedding model is unavailable). The AI judges each numbered criterion from those passages and the abstract (criteria before and after the passages), quoting a passage. An exclusion needs a criterion not met with a quotation found in the full text; the first criterion not met in the reason order is the primary reason. Two readings per report; conflicts and every proposed exclusion or unclear report go to the reviewer. The fuzzy controller of Rahgozar and Mortezaagha is not used: in their evaluation an all-criteria rule failed half of the true includes.

    • Rahgozar and Mortezaagha (2026), Artif Intell Rev: An auditable pipeline for fuzzy full-text screening — chunking, contrastive semantic highlighting, LLM judgment and the audit trail.
    • Cochrane Handbook v6.5.1, Chapter 4 §4.6 — two reviewers at full text (MECIR C39), reasons for exclusion (C41), linking reports (C42), first unmet criterion as the primary reason.
    • PRISMA 2020 — reports sought, not retrieved, assessed, excluded with reasons; studies included.
    • Cao et al. (2025), Ann Intern Med — criteria before and after the article, numbered per-criterion reasoning.
    • Flemyng et al. (2025), position statement on AI in evidence synthesis — human oversight and reporting of AI use.
