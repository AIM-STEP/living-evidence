AIM-STEP overview

1. Purpose

Clarify what matters.

AIM-STEP helps researchers and clinicians move from scattered literature to focused, clinically relevant questions, so decisions are grounded in evidence rather than uncertainty.

2. AI for evidence based medicine

Support trustworthy synthesis.

By combining clinical reasoning with AI-assisted evidence analysis, AIM-STEP helps identify high-quality evidence, reduce noise, and improve how findings are interpreted in medical practice.

3. STEP

Simple · Transparent · Exact · Prompt (STEP)

The STEP framework organizes the pathway from searching and screening to extraction, appraisal, and recommendation, making evidence-based decisions more structured and easier to trust.
