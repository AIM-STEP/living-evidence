About AIM-STEP

From literature to living evidence.

AIM-STEP sits at the intersection of evidence-based medicine, digital health, and methodological rigor. It is designed to make evidence synthesis more structured, reproducible, and clinically interpretable.

01

Systematic

A clear process for literature identification and study selection across complex clinical questions.

02

Transparent

Evidence workflow

The AIM-STEP process.

Each stage turns large and fragmented evidence into a structured, clinically interpretable recommendation pathway.

Step 01

文献筛选

Define the clinical question and identify relevant studies through targeted, reproducible search strategies.

  • Eligibility criteria
  • Search strategy
  • Study selection
Step 02

数据提取

Capture study characteristics, outcomes, and relevant variables in a consistent and structured format.

  • PICO elements
  • Outcome definitions
  • Design variables
Step 03

数据分析

Quantify patterns, heterogeneity, and effect estimates to understand the evidence base with clarity.

  • Effect size synthesis
  • Heterogeneity check
  • Subgroup analysis
Step 04

GRADE评估

Assess certainty of evidence across domains such as risk of bias, inconsistency, and indirectness.

  • Risk of bias
  • Certainty rating
  • Evidence quality
Step 05

证据推荐

Translate the synthesized findings into recommendations that support clinicians and health systems.

  • Clinical impact
  • Recommendation strength
  • Decision support
