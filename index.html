AIM-STEP

Simple · Transparent · Exact · Prompt

Each value in the AIM-STEP framework reflects a practical principle for making evidence synthesis clearer, more reproducible, and more clinically actionable.

01

Simple

Reduce complexity by making the workflow clear, structured, and easy to follow from literature to recommendation.

02

Transparent

Make the evidence path visible, traceable, and open to review at every stage of synthesis and appraisal.

03

Exact

Focus on precise questions, explicit criteria, and careful extraction so clinical conclusions remain evidence-based.

04

Prompt

Use clear prompts and guided reasoning to improve consistency, reproducibility, and interpretability in AI-assisted reviews.
