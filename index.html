A disciplined workflow.

Each step is designed to reduce noise, strengthen quality, and improve decision confidence.

Step 01

Search

Define the clinical question and identify relevant studies.

Step 02

Screen

Apply explicit eligibility criteria to keep the review focused.

Step 03

Extract

Capture outcomes, variables, and study-level evidence.

Step 04

Appraise

Assess bias, certainty, and methodological quality.

Step 05

Recommend

Translate findings into clear, actionable clinical guidance.
