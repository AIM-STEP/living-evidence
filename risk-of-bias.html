Toolset / Risk of bias assessment
← Back: Extraction

Included studies

  1. Check each study's design, and choose the tool for each design (the recommended one is first; every study of that design uses the same tool).
  2. Start the AI pre-fill: the local model drafts every answer with a quotation from the full text. Drafts stay hidden.
  3. Open each result, answer on your own, then compare with the AI draft, resolve the conflicts and confirm.
Outcome results to assess

Y yes · PY probably yes · PN probably no · N no · NI no information. Questions whose condition is not met are NA. Domain judgements follow the tool's algorithm where it has one; you can change a judgement with a reason.
